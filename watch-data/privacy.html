Privacy Policy — Watch Data Pull

Effective 18 October 2026. About the app

Who uses the app

One person: the owner of the Google Cloud project the app runs under. The app is not offered to anyone else.

What the app reads

With the owner's consent, the app has read-only access, through the Google Health API, to the owner's own:

  • sleep;
  • activity and fitness, such as steps and exercise sessions;
  • health metrics and measurements, such as heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen and skin temperature;
  • irregular rhythm notifications and ECG recordings;
  • mindfulness sessions.

The app never writes to, changes or deletes any Google data.

How the data is used

Once a day the app files a summary in the owner's private notes, kept in a private repository the owner controls. The owner, and the owner's own assistant tools acting for the owner, use the summary to plan the owner's sleep, exercise and health. The data is used for nothing else.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to anyone.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Sign-in tokens are kept in an access-controlled credential store, never in the notes.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored summaries from the notes.

Contact

Questions go to the user support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
